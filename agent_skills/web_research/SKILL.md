# Web Research Skill

Use sources returned by Gemini's web search as research leads. Preserve each
source title and URL accurately when citing it. Do not create citations or
claim to have read a full article when only a title or snippet is available.

For each proposed idea, distinguish:

- What the supplied research directly supports.
- What is an editorial suggestion or inference.
- What needs further source verification.

If the results do not describe specific incidents, say so and propose
research directions rather than presenting incidents as established facts.
